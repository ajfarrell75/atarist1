Atari TOS images
----------------
⚠ `roms/tos102uk.img` and `roms/tos162uk.img`, when present in this package, are
**original Atari TOS ROMs**. They are NOT free software and NOT covered by the GPL:
they remain the property of their rights holders and are included only for the ST /
STE machine profiles. NeoST does not need them — it boots EmuTOS out of the box.

To build a package without them, set `NEOST_PACKAGE_NO_ATARI_TOS=1` in the environment
before running `packaging/stage_free_data.sh` (see that script's header).

Reference emulators
-------------------
Hatari (GPLv2+, https://framagit.org/hatari/hatari) and MAME are used as behavioural
*references* — read as sources, and run as an oracle during development. No part of
either is redistributed in this package.

--- stb_image (extern/stb/stb_image.h) ---
stb_image v2.30 — Sean Barrett et contributeurs. Double licence au choix : MIT ou domaine
public (Unlicense). Utilisé par le GUI pour décoder la photo du clavier (pic/).
https://github.com/nothings/stb

--- libmt32emu / Munt (optional, dynamically linked) ---
Munt MT-32/CM-32L emulator, LGPL 2.1 (https://github.com/munt/munt). Used by the macOS GUI
when present at build time (brew install mt32emu) to render the ST's MIDI OUT through a
Roland MT-32/CM-32L. The Roland ROMs are NOT included: the user provides them (roms/mt32/).
